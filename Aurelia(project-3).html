GET TO KNOW YOUR LABEL

Ingredient library.

A quick introduction to ingredients
commonly found in skincare.

01

Hyaluronic Acid

A humectant that attracts and holds water, helping skin feel hydrated and plump.

OFTEN USED FOR · HYDRATION
02

Niacinamide

A form of vitamin B3 used in skincare to support the skin barrier and improve the look of uneven tone.

OFTEN USED FOR · BARRIER SUPPORT
03

Salicylic Acid

A beta hydroxy acid (BHA) that helps exfoliate inside pores and is often used for blemish-prone skin.

OFTEN USED FOR · CLOGGED PORES
04

Vitamin C

An antioxidant ingredient often included in routines to help improve the appearance of dullness and uneven tone.

OFTEN USED FOR · BRIGHT-LOOKING SKIN
05

Ceramides

Lipids naturally found in the skin barrier. Skincare products use them to help maintain moisture and barrier function.

OFTEN USED FOR · MOISTURE BARRIER
06

Aloe Vera

A botanical ingredient often used in skincare formulas for its soothing and conditioning properties.

OFTEN USED FOR · SKIN COMFORT

Ingredient information is general education, not medical advice. Introduce new products carefully and patch test when appropriate.

EVERY SKIN IS DIFFERENT

Get to know
your skin type.

Your skin can change with weather, age, environment and other factors. These common descriptions are a starting point for understanding what your skin may need.

NormalGenerally balanced, without persistent excess oil or dryness.
DryMay feel tight, rough or flaky and often benefits from richer moisturising care.
OilyOften has visible shine and excess sebum, especially around the T-zone.
CombinationMay feel oily in some areas and dry or comfortable in others.
SensitiveMay react easily with stinging, redness or discomfort to some products.
A SIMPLE PLACE TO BEGIN

Build an everyday routine.

You don't need a complicated shelf. Start with a few basics, introduce products gradually and pay attention to how your skin responds.

AM

Morning

Gentle cleanse (as needed), moisturiser and broad-spectrum sunscreen.

PM

Evening

Remove makeup and sunscreen, cleanse gently, then moisturise.

GO SLOW

New products

Introduce one new product at a time and follow its usage instructions.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Quick answers to common beginner skincare questions.

A simple routine usually includes cleansing, moisturising and daytime sun protection. You can add other products based on your needs.

Daily broad-spectrum sun protection is a helpful habit. Follow product directions and reapply as directed, particularly after swimming, sweating or towel drying.

Some combinations may irritate skin, especially when first starting out. Introduce one active at a time, follow product instructions and ask a dermatologist if unsure.

Seek professional advice for persistent irritation, painful or severe acne, sudden skin changes, rashes or concerns that do not improve.
